# 07 - Uncertainties And Conflicts

## Chua The Ket Luan Chac Chan

1. Dieu kien du thi cuoi ky:
   Co bang diem ghi `Khong du DKDT`/`KD DK`, nhung chua thay quy tac dinh luong. Khong nen suy luan tu diem 0 TBKT/BTL hoac vang.

2. Diem liet thanh phan:
   Chua thay tai lieu noi co/khong co diem liet. Bang diem co sinh vien diem THI 1 van tinh tong ket F, nhung khong du de ket luan quy tac diem liet.

3. Quy tac lam tron:
   Chua thay quy dinh. Excel hien thi so thap phan noi nhu `5.300000000000001`; day la dau vet tinh toan, khong phai quy tac lam tron.

4. Duoc dung tai lieu/Internet/IDE trong KTHP:
   Chua thay nguon chinh thuc. Ghi chu sinh vien noi dung NetBeans, JDK, client co giam sat, nhung khong noi duoc dung tai lieu hay Internet.

5. Thoi luong KTHP 2025-2026:
   File 2025-2026 noi KTHP 6 bai nhung khong ghi thoi gian. De cuong 2024-2025 ghi 60 phut cho KTHP 5 bai; khong duoc gan mac 60 phut cho ban moi neu chua xac minh.

6. So lan submit/gioi han submit/diem tung test:
   Chua co nguon.

7. Console output:
   Co suy luan manh rang server cham du lieu submit, khong cham console. Ghi chu noi `toString` chi de xem va nop object chu khong in ra man hinh. Tuy nhien chua co quy dinh chinh thuc.
