## Dieu Kien Du Thi Va Qua Mon

- Co bang chung mot so sinh vien bi `Khong du DKDT` trong `THI_KTHP_CQ.xlsx`, va `Khong du DKDT`/`KD DK` trong bang diem thanh phan/tong ket 2024-2025.
- Chua tim thay quy dinh dinh luong chinh thuc, vi du vang bao nhieu buoi, diem thanh phan toi thieu bao nhieu, hay diem liet thanh phan.
- Chua tim thay quy tac lam tron chinh thuc. Bang diem hien thi gia tri thap phan nhu `5.300000000000001` trong Excel, nen khong du bang chung ve lam tron hien thi/cuoi cung.
- Dieu kien qua mon theo diem chu co trong bang tong ket, nhung nguong quy doi diem chu khong nam trong tai lieu INT1433 da doc.

## Khac Biet Theo Nam/Ky

- 2024-2025: cuoi ky 5 bai, 60 phut, thang 0 bai = 0 diem.
- 2025-2026: cuoi ky 6 bai, co du TCP/UDP/RMI/WS, 0 bai AC = 1 diem.
- 2025-2026 tach TBKT va BTL thanh online 20% + truc tiep 80%; de cuong 2024-2025 khong mo ta chi tiet online/truc tiep nhu vay.
- File `Diem thi Lap trinh mang - D22.pdf` ghi `Thi lan 1 - Hoc ky I - Nam hoc 2025-2026` va cac tieu chi bao ve du an/bai bao cao/implementation, mau thuan voi file danh gia Dong 2025-2026 neu coi la KTHP AC. Can xac minh xem day la bang diem thanh phan/du an hay mot hinh thuc thi rieng.
