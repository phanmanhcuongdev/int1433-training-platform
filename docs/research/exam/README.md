## Ket Luan Nhanh

Ket luan manh nhat tim duoc:

- Cau truc diem lap lai trong de cuong 2024-2025, bang diem 2024-2025 va tai lieu danh gia Dong 2025-2026: `CC 10% + TBKT/Thuc hanh 20% + BTL 20% + Thi KTHP 50%`.
- Ban moi nhat co noi ro hinh thuc danh gia la `Danh gia, cho diem LTM Ky dong 2025.docx`: thi KTHP truc tiep tai phong may A3, 6 bai, gom it nhat 1 TCP, 1 UDP, 1 RMI, 1 Web Service, 2 bai con lai co the thuoc bat ky dang nao, cham theo so bai AC.
- De cuong 2024-2025 cu hon noi thi KTHP 5 bai trong 60 phut tai phong may; thang diem chung 0/1/2/3/4/5 bai dung -> 0/4/6/8/9/10.
- Tai lieu 2025-2026 doi thang diem: 0/1/2/3/4/5/6 bai AC -> 1/4/6/7/8/9/10.
- Co bang diem/chung cu 2024-2025 cho thay sinh vien co the bi `Khong du DKDT`, `KD DK`, `Vang thi`, `Bo thi`, nhung chua tim thay dieu kien dinh luong chinh thuc de du thi.
