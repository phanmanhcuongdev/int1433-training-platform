# 05 - MVP Scope

## Must-have

- Content-only site hoac Markdown catalog.
- Taxonomy va filter: protocol, stream/service, level, source label.
- 48 bai Balanced MVP.
- Moi bai co statement, contract, common failures, hint, starter code note.
- 3 mock exam blueprint, chua can online timer.
- Traceability toi claim/source label.
- Download local starter/harness cho it nhat 6 bai pilot.

## Should-have

- Search theo tag.
- Checklist loi hay gap.
- Local judge cho TCP Byte, TCP Character, UDP String, UDP Object, RMI Data, WS Data.
- Result vocabulary thong nhat: AC/WA/PE/TLE/RE.
- Markdown/YAML validation script.

## Must-not-build-yet

- Online judge production.
- Multi-language submission.
- Kubernetes.
- Distributed judge cluster.
- Anti-cheat/proctoring.
- Complex leaderboard.
- AI recommendation.
- Realtime collaboration.
- Classroom dashboard.

## MVP Size Options

| Phuong an | Foundation | TCP | UDP | RMI | WS | Debug | Mock | Tong bai | Khi nao dung |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Minimal MVP | 4 | 6 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 24 | Can demo nhanh trong 1-2 tuan |
| Balanced Curriculum | 8 | 12 | 8 | 6 | 6 | 8 | 3 | 48 | De xuat chinh |
| Full Bank | 16 | 32 | 24 | 16 | 16 | 20 | 10 | 124 | Sau khi judge va content pipeline on |

De xuat chon Balanced Curriculum. Ly do:

- Du bao phu du 4 cong nghe KTHP 6 bai.
- TCP khong vuot 40% tong bai.
- Co du debugging de tranh hoc tu.
- Chua qua lon den muc kho bao tri.

## Tieu Chi Hoan Thanh MVP

- 100% bai co `source_label`.
- 100% Exam Track bai co `source_claim_ids` hoac ghi ro EXTENDED.
- Co it nhat 1 bai cho moi nhom: TCP Byte/Data/Character/Object, UDP String/Data/Object, RMI Data/Object, WS Data/Object.
- Co it nhat 8 bai debugging.
- Co 3 mock exam blueprint hop le.
- Co validation schema chay qua tat ca bai.
- Co local harness pilot cho 6 bai truoc khi ban ve online judge.
